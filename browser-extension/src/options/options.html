The PIN is mandatory: protection refuses to start without one, and it is asked for every time something would weaken it. It cannot be recovered - clearing this extension's data is the only reset. It is not a lock on the browser, which can still disable the extension: it is a speed bump for the moment you want to switch it off.

PIN & commitment

Removing the PIN switches protection off and releases the commitment lock, because filtering with no PIN behind it is filtering you can talk yourself out of. The setup screen comes back until a new PIN exists.

About

What this extension cannot do: it only sees this browser. Other browsers, apps, and anything on your phone are out of its reach, and it cannot put a family resolver behind your back the way the Android app can. On Android, the app is the real thing; here it is the same lists in front of one browser.
